Botox & Dysport

Both Botox & Dysport are neuromodulators that effectively treat dynamic wrinkles — those that form when we make facial movements such as squinting, frowning, and raising our eyebrows. Neuromodulators also have medical applications, such as the treatment of migraines, hyperhidrosis and TMJ (jaw clenching).

Neuromodulators

Botox or Dysport are neuromodulators which temporarily weaken the activity of the muscles that cause expression lines to form over time. Injections result in temporarily reduced or fully blocked muscle activity.

You will begin to notice a visible smoothing of your crow's feet lines, frown lines between your brows, and forehead lines within 3-5 days after treatment.

Botox and Dysport can also be used to improve symptoms associated with migraines, TMJ and hyperhidrosis. Consultations are required.

What to Expect

Consultations are free. During this consultation appointment, you will speak with a Nurse Injector, who will assess your treatment goals, medical history and budget.

Your Professional Nurse Injector will then help you achieve your desired results — whether it is a subtle or full reduction in visible lines and wrinkles for a natural and refreshed look.
