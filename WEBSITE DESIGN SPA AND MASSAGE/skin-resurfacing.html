Sublative Skin Resurfacing

Sublative Skin Rejuvenation uses fractionated bi-polar radio frequency (RF) to penetrate through the skin. RF energy generates fractional deep dermal heating to induce skin injury and maximize collagen production — while keeping the top layer of the skin intact, leading to a rejuvenated appearance.

Skin Resurfacing

Sublative Skin Resurfacing is an FDA-approved treatment that gently resurfaces the skin over consecutive treatments (3-5 recommended).

Sublative is known to have results with the following:

  • Brightening the skin
  • Shrinking large pores
  • Improving skin texture
  • Tightening loose skin
  • Reducing the appearance of acne scarring and minor wrinkles

What to Expect

During treatment, intense heat is generated under the surface of the skin without disrupting the skin's surface barrier in the process.

Sublative eMatrix technology stimulates collagen and elastin production in the deep layers of the skin, resulting in a tighter, smoother, more refined and brighter appearance to areas treated.

Downtime is approximately 48 hours with mild redness remaining for 3 to 5 days (makeup is not recommended for the first 48 hours). Results continue to improve over 3-5 months after the final treatment and can be maintained annually.
