Aesthetic Services

Manicures & Pedicures

Our licensed pedicure lounge is set in a relaxing atmosphere with calming music where our professionals will pamper you with a luxurious treatment. For a manicure or pedicure, they provide a deep clean for your nails, address any nail health concerns, and ensure you leave looking great. Regular and gel polish (Shellac) options available.

Spa Pedicure

Our base-line pedicure, nothing short of extraordinary.

Treat yourself to a hot or cold beverage while you enjoy a warm foot bath. After dead skin removal, nail and cuticle maintenance, your aesthetician will treat your toes to a luxurious sugar scrub and a relaxing foot/calf massage. Finish off with the polish application of your choice.

Add-ons: Gel Polish, Paraffin Mask, Hot Stone Foot Massage.

Charcoal Detox Pedicure

Sit back and relax in a warm foot bath featuring a detoxifying charcoal foot soak. This pedicure includes nail/cuticle maintenance, foot filing and regular nail polish application of your choice. You will also receive a charcoal exfoliating foot scrub and a detoxifying charcoal foot masque.

Black charcoal powder activates to attract and eliminate dirt, oil, and odor-causing bacteria from deep within the pores. At the end of this pedicure, smoother, healthier, more rejuvenated feet are revealed.

Ultimate Spa Pedicure

Treat your toes to a luxurious experience through our Ultimate Pedicure.

This pedicure comes equipped with everything to make your feet brand new. Featuring foot/nail maintenance, sugar scrub and everything in our Spa Pedicure, with all the add-ons already included — a Hot Stone Foot Massage, Paraffin Mask and Gel Polish application.

Also includes your choice of an alcoholic/non-alcoholic beverage from our drink menu.

Flash Pedi

A Flash Pedi is the option suited for clients on the run.

Includes all of the important maintenance of a regular pedicure, sped up for your convenience. Polish application is determined by you — no polish, regular or gel!

Please note, Flash Pedis do not include a foot massage or sugar scrub.

Spa Manicure

Treat yourself to a deep clean and polish the appearance of your hands in one treatment.

Our Spa Manicure includes filing and shaping of the nails, cuticle maintenance, a soothing hand massage and polish application of your choice.

Add-ons: Gel Polish, Paraffin Mask.
